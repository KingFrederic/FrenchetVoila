# 🦊 Les Couleurs — a 5-minute French mini-lesson

**Subject:** First colours in French · **Learner:** one 5-year-old, no French background
**Duration:** ~5 minutes · **Guide:** *Léo le Renard* (Léo the Fox)
**Goal:** by the end, the child can hear a colour in French and point to it, and can
say *Rouge, Bleu, Jaune, Vert* out loud with a smile.

This is the script I would actually teach, mapped onto the website so a parent,
tutor, or classroom can run it live. Teach it warmly, slowly, and with lots of
"Bravo!" — at five, **confidence is the lesson**; the vocabulary rides along with it.

---

## The four colours

| French | Say it like | English | Picture anchor | Bonus word |
|--------|-------------|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Rouge** | *roozh* | Red | 🍓 strawberry | *une fraise* |
| **Bleu** | *bluh* | Blue | 🐳 whale | *une baleine* |
| **Jaune** | *zhohn* | Yellow | ☀️ sun | *le soleil* |
| **Vert** | *vair* | Green | 🐸 frog | *une grenouille* |

> Why these four? They're the highest-contrast starter set (no blue/green or
> red/orange confusion), they map onto things a five-year-old already loves, and
> four is the sweet spot — enough to feel like an achievement, few enough to
> actually remember.

## Minute-by-minute

### ⏱ 0:00–0:45 — Bonjour! (warm-up)
- Open the site. Léo waves: **"Bonjour!"** Wave back together.
- *"Today we learn colours — in French! Ready? On y va!"* (*let's go*)
- Press **Commencer**. Keep it silly and low-stakes.

### ⏱ 0:45–2:30 — Les Couleurs (flash cards)
The cards start showing only the **French word**. This is the magic move:
> *"This card says* **Rouge**. *What colour do you think is hiding? … Let's check!"*
- Child taps the card. It **flips to reveal the colour**, the picture, and Léo
  says the word. Celebrate the guess whether right or not.
- Repeat for all four. Each reveal, **say it together** twice: *"Rouge… rouge!"*
- Tie each colour to its picture: *"Rouge, like une fraise — a strawberry!"*
- When all four are flipped, Léo cheers **"Bravo!"** and the game button wiggles.

**Teacher tip:** cover the word with your thumb and ask *"Quelle couleur?"* to
turn the reveal into a mini guessing game. That single interaction is the whole
pedagogy: predict → reveal → confirm.

### ⏱ 2:30–4:30 — Le Jeu (listen & find)
- Press **On joue?** Four colour squares appear, no words.
- Léo asks out loud: **"Trouve… Rouge!"** (*Find red!*)
- Child taps the matching square. Correct → confetti + **"Oui!"**; a miss just
  shakes gently and says **"Essaie encore!"** (*try again*) — no losing, ever.
- Four rounds, one per colour. Stars fill up as they go.

### ⏱ 4:30–5:00 — Bravo! (close)
- Celebration screen with the score and confetti.
- Wrap up: *"You learned four colours in French today — Rouge, Bleu, Jaune, Vert!
  Give me a high five! Au revoir!"* (*goodbye*)
- Optional: **Rejouer** to play once more, or **Recommencer** to start over.

## Teaching moves that make it land

- **Say it, don't spell it.** Five-year-olds learn sound first. Every colour is
  spoken by Léo; echo it together, exaggerate the mouth shape.
- **Anchor to a picture.** "Bleu like the whale" sticks far better than "bleu = blue".
- **No wrong answers.** Wrong taps are gentle nudges, never buzzers. Praise the
  attempt: *"Presque! Try the yellow one."*
- **Movement helps.** Have them point at something *rouge* in the real room after
  the card reveals it.
- **Keep it to 5 minutes.** Stop while it's still fun. Come back tomorrow.

## Stretch it further (if they're hungry for more)
- Add *"C'est quelle couleur?"* pointing at their clothes.
- Count the colours: *un, deux, trois, quatre*.
- Sing it: "Rouge, bleu, jaune, vert" to any tune they like.

## Assessment (how you know it worked)
- ✅ Can point to the right square when Léo names a colour (the game already checks this).
- ✅ Can say at least two of the four colour words unprompted.
- ✅ Wants to play again. At five, that's the real test.
